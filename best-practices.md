# RJ Plumbing Homepage: Best-Practices Brief (for Monday)

**Goal:** a homepage that gets Clark County homeowners to **call or book** in under 10 seconds, in RJ's own voice, with the clean look Kay likes on Sunrise Plumbing Pros.

## What Sunrise does well (and why the client likes it)
- **Proof at the very top:** a thin bar with star rating + review count, "Licensed MP-208555 & Insured," financing, and service-area count before you read anything else.
- **One clear headline + two buttons:** "Trusted Plumbing Service for Savannah, GA," then **Call Now** / Explore Services. Few words, lots of white space.
- **Short trust tiles** right under the hero (Licensed, Fast Response, Residential & Commercial, Financing).
- **Offers and service areas in the nav.** Every city gets its own page, which is good for local SEO.
- **Real numbers, real license number.** It feels specific, not generic.

## What to keep from each
- **A:** split hero (message + real team photo) is the backbone of the sample.
- **B:** the local-photo feel, used in service cards and the "Why RJ" section instead of a full-bleed hero.
- **C:** phone-first priority. A big brass **Call** button is the primary CTA, and a sticky Call/Book bar on mobile.
- **All three:** Rainier Slate palette with brass for actions only, the 8-item nav, the brass Emergency link, and the 4-card Specialties strip (clog clearing, drain cleaning, augering, camera inspections). These are good calls by the designer; keep them.

## Recommendations the account manager can voice
1. **Write with RJ's real words, not lorem.** RJ already has strong copy: "Clark County's Top Residential Plumbers," "good old-fashioned, honest service," "from the ground up." The client should react to their own voice on Monday, not to a layout.
2. **Add a Sunrise-style proof bar above the header:** Google stars + review count, "Licensed, Bonded & Insured," hours, and financing. Use the real WA license # RJPLUPS797M8.
3. **Make the phone call the #1 action.** Use a brass click-to-call in the hero and header, plus a **sticky Call / Book bar on mobile**. Most plumbing visitors are on a phone.
4. **Put real Google reviews on the homepage**, with 3 quotes plus the rating. RJ's current site loads reviews through a widget that shows nothing useful. Pull them from the Google Business Profile.
5. **Show the whole service area** (15 Clark County towns, from RJ's FAQ) as chips now, then build city pages later like Sunrise does.
6. **Book through one Jobber button, not an on-page form (C).** Two booking paths split leads and confuse people.
7. **Get a half-day real photo shoot** of the team, van and jobs. This beats stock and is the biggest step up from Sunrise. Keep the page fast: compressed images, no sliders or heavy widgets.
